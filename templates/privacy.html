Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account and log your expenses. This includes your email address, password, and the financial transaction details (amount, category, date, and description) that you enter into the application.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the core functionality of Spendly. We use your information to maintain your account, store your expense logs, and generate your spending reports. We do not use your financial data for marketing or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party companies. Any service providers we may use for hosting are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing infrastructure services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of your account and data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.com.
